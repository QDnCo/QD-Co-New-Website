## How it is built
- Iteration 1 = the original pages from commit `f5f2b9d`, regenerated by `patch_iteration1.py` into `/iteration-1/` (base href `/`, links kept inside the folder).
- Iterations 2–10 share the root pages; `?iter=N` selects one and is remembered in localStorage. `v3.jsx` (`QD_ITERS`) picks the hero and services layout; `v4.css` is the shared base (Iteration 2 tokens); `iterations.css` holds the design language of 3–10.
- Every iteration supports light and dark, India and Dubai.
